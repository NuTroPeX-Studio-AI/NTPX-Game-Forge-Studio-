## Increment

Adds the first TypeScript-specific parser adapter foundation behind the verified provider-neutral `code-intelligence-syntax` boundary.

`TypeScriptParserAdapter` emits deterministic top-level declaration evidence for supported functions, classes, interfaces, type aliases, enums, namespaces, and `const`/`let`/`var` declarations. Evidence records stable kind, name, source line, ordinal, and SHA-256 correlation output. Top-level brace tracking shields line comments, block comments, quoted strings, and multiline template strings so their braces do not alter structural depth, and nested declarations are not promoted to top-level evidence.
